# How-humans-taught-machines-to-think
Discover five foundational research papers that transformed AI from an academic dream into one of humanity's most powerful technologies

**About the project**
* When exploring modern AI, most beginners get caught between extremes:
1. high-level tutorials that hide the underlying mechanics,
2. bottoms up 'ask AI to learn AI', or
3. academic papers packed with dense notation

All the three approaches obscure intuition building

This book bridges that gap by going back to the foundational literature that started it all. It translates five landmark research papers into intuitive mental models, clear mathematical walkthroughs, and step-by-step visual diagrams.

**Note to parents & educators:**
* This open-access educational resource is authored by a 13-year-old student researcher and managed in partnership with adult mentors to maintain a safe, welcoming learning environment for young AI explorers.

**Who is this for?**
* **High School & AP Computer Science Students:** Looking to go beyond basic coding syntax and understand how neural networks actually compute.
* **Undergraduate Beginners:** Seeking a visual, conceptual companion guide before tackling primary literature in coursework.
* **Educators & STEM Club Leaders:** Looking for free, modular supplementary reading materials for AI and robotics programs.
